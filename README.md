# ECON1626 Labour Policy Proposal

This repository contains Michael Zappone's Assessment 2 policy proposal for ECON1626 Economics of Artificial Intelligence.

## Repository contents

- [`proposal.md`](proposal.md): policy proposal to the Australian Government Department of Employment and Workplace Relations
- [`reflection.md`](reflection.md): reflection on the use of artificial intelligence during preparation

## Policy focus

The proposal responds to the Australian Government's National AI Plan and the Department of Employment and Workplace Relations' 2026 monitoring report on AI and employment. It recommends a two-year, employer-linked training and worker-governance pilot for occupations showing credible signs of AI-related task displacement.

## Navigation

Start with `proposal.md`. The proposal includes its policy-client statement, executive summary, economic analysis, options matrix, implementation plan, risks and RMIT Harvard reference list. Read `reflection.md` separately; it is excluded from the proposal word count.
